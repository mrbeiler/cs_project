THE CRYPT

Your lantern lights only a small circle, and walls swallow the light. Search the chambers for 3 key fragments to open the door at the end. Climb into a candle-lit coffin and hold still when he comes. Cracked floor slabs crunch, rats squeak and bats screech when you step on or bump them — every noise draws him. He can see your lantern flame from far away, even through a coffin lid, so snuff it when you hide.

Move: WASD or arrows. Hide or climb out: E. Lantern on/off: Space.

“It's high noon somewhere.”
